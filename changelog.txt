[B]0.2.0[/B]
• Adds a "Recent queues" entry to Kodi's music library, which is what puts it in the music row on the home screen. This works in any skin and needs no skin changes
• Selecting it opens a list of the recorded queues; picking one restores it, exactly as the pop-up does
• The pop-up is unchanged and still what you get from running the add-on
• If this profile has no music library entries of its own yet, Kodi's defaults are copied in first, so Artists, Albums and the rest are not lost
• Can be turned off in settings, which removes the entry again

[B]0.1.1[/B]
• The "Recent queues" button in the skin's music row no longer disappears. It used to hide itself while the history was empty, which relied on the add-on keeping a value alive on the home screen — and on Android that value is lost every time Kodi goes to the background, so the button went missing and stayed missing
• The button is now shown whenever the add-on is installed. Opening it with nothing recorded says so

[B]0.1.0[/B]
• First release. Records the music queue every time it is taken away — when a new album or playlist replaces it, when a video interrupts it, when playback is stopped, and when Kodi quits
• Each entry remembers which track was playing and how far into it, and restores to exactly that point rather than starting the track again
• Pick from the "Recent queues" list: album and artist, how many tracks and how many unplayed, how long ago it was played and where it stopped
• Playing the same album twice leaves two entries, and restoring one leaves it on the list, so nothing is lost by choosing the wrong one
• Reached from the "Recent queues" button in the skin's music row, or by running the add-on
• Settings for how many queues to keep, whether to restore the last one when Kodi starts, and whether restored queues start playing or wait paused
