v0.2.0
• The "Recent queues" button can now be placed on the skin's home screen from the add-on settings: the running skin is edited so its music categories row gets a button that opens the pop-up directly, with no window change
• Estuary and Contuary are supported. The edit is reversible from the same settings page, and a skin update that wipes it is put right by running it again
• When Kodi's bundled skin cannot be edited in place, an editable copy is set up in the user's add-ons and the button added there

v0.1.0
• First release. Records the music queue every time it is taken away — when a new album or playlist replaces it, when a video interrupts it, when playback is stopped, and when Kodi quits
• Each entry remembers which track was playing and how far into it, and restores to exactly that point rather than starting the track again
• Pick from the "Recent queues" list: album and artist, how many tracks and how many unplayed, how long ago it was played and where it stopped
• Playing the same album twice leaves two entries, and restoring one leaves it on the list, so nothing is lost by choosing the wrong one
• Reached from the "Recent queues" button in the skin's music row, or by running the add-on
• Settings for how many queues to keep, whether to restore the last one when Kodi starts, and whether restored queues start playing or wait paused
